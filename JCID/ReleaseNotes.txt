	<h3>About the JCID Admin module</h3>
	<p class="Owner">
		<b>Version 1.0.0</b><br /><br />
	</p>
	<p>Management side of the Judicial Center Identification (JCID) application. Moved here from the public (jud12) site, which keeps the applicant form, replacement form and payment pages.</p>

	<h4>Features</h4>
	<ul>
		<li>List of all JCID applications and the credit-card payments (filterable by date range)</li>
		<li>Review / edit an application: applicant details, workflow dates, mark picked up</li>
		<li>Reject an application with a reason and email the applicant a correction link</li>
		<li>Copy a payment link for an applicant who still owes payment</li>
		<li>Link targets on the public site are built from the External URL module setting</li>
	</ul>

	<h4>Notes / prerequisites</h4>
	<ul>
		<li>Reads and writes the jud12 site's database through the <b>Jud12</b> connection string, which must be present in this site's web.config connectionStrings.</li>
		<li>SSNs are stored encrypted. The <b>JCID_SSN_EncryptionKey</b> appSetting in this site's web.config must match the value on the public JCID site, or SSNs will not decrypt.</li>
		<li>Applicant photos live on the jud12 site; set the External URL in the module settings so they display.</li>
	</ul>
