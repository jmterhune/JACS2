	<h3>About the Transcriptionist Admin module</h3>
	<p class="Owner">
		<b>Version 1.0.0</b><br /><br />
	</p>
	<p>Management side of the Court Approved Transcriptionist application. Moved here from the public (jud12) site, which keeps the applicant form and the directory.</p>

	<h4>Features</h4>
	<ul>
		<li>Dashboard with this year's application counts and the latest pending applications</li>
		<li>Searchable list of all applications</li>
		<li>Application detail, edit (including experience records), approve, reject and delete</li>
		<li>Approve / reject emails to the applicant</li>
		<li>Link to the public application page, set in the module settings</li>
	</ul>

	<h4>Notes</h4>
	<ul>
		<li>Reads and writes the jud12 site's database through the Jud12 connection string.</li>
		<li>Uploaded documents live on the jud12 site; they are linked from there and cannot be added or removed here.</li>
	</ul>
