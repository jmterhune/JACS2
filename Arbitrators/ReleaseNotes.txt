	<h3>About Arbitrators</h3>
	<p class="Owner">
		<b>Version 00.00.01</b><br /><br />
	</p>
	<p>Internal review sister to the public-facing Arbitrators module on jud12.flcourts.org. Reviewers open a
	specific application from the link in the notification email (the "aid" query parameter), view the
	applicant's details and uploaded documents, and approve or deny the application. The applicant is emailed
	the result.</p>

	<h4>Features</h4>
	<ul>
		<li>Application list (DataTables): filter by status, magnifier opens the review view.</li>
		<li>Review view: applicant details, attachments (Training Certificate, Bar Card), Approve / Deny with review notes.</li>
		<li>Approval/denial emails the applicant automatically.</li>
	</ul>
