Court Calendar (intranet authoring) - Release Notes

00.00.01
- Initial release.
- Admins manage the judge roster (assigning each judge's intranet user and JA user), divisions (name, county, layout type, assigned judge, Zoom/contact details, URL slug) and seeded/custom categories with colors.
- Assigned judges and JAs manage their division's monthly calendars: add week-row headers; add/edit entries (timespan or all-day, short title, detail, category); strike/unstrike items; set title, notes and published state.
- "Send update notification" emails calendar subscribers.
- Reads and writes the public site's tjc_cc_* tables through the "Jud12" connection; the intranet site's root web.config must define that connection string.
