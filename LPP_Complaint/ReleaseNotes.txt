LPP Complaint (intranet administration) - Release Notes

00.00.01
- Initial release.
- Lists every Local Professionalism Panel complaint submitted on the public site, newest first, filterable by handling status and searchable in the table itself.
- Opens a complaint in full: referring party, attorney being referred, the ticked nature-of-incident boxes, the alleged violation and the electronic signature.
- Users with module edit permission set a handling status (New, Under Review, Resolved, Closed) with notes, and can delete a complaint.
- Exports whatever the status filter is showing to CSV.
- Reads and writes the public site's tjc_modules_lpp_complaints and tjc_modules_lpp_complaint_natures tables through the "Jud12" connection; the intranet site's root web.config must define that connection string.
